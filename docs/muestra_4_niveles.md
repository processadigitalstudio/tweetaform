# A1-01 — Nice to Meet You!

**Nivel:** A1  
**Gramática:** Verb to be: afirmativo, negativo y preguntas  
**Vocabulario:** Saludos, presentaciones, informacion personal  
**Can-do:** Puede saludar, presentarse, decir quien es y de donde es

### Lección 1 · Vocabulario

| Palabra/frase | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Hello / Hi | Hola | Hello! How are you? |
| Good morning / afternoon / evening | Buenos dias / tardes / noches | Good morning, everyone. |
| Nice to meet you | Mucho gusto | Nice to meet you, Daniel. |
| My name is... | Mi nombre es... | My name is Camila. |
| What's your name? | ¿Cual es tu nombre? | Hi, what's your name? |
| I'm from... | Soy de... | I'm from Cartagena. |
| Where are you from? | ¿De donde eres? | Where are you from, Marta? |
| Teacher | Profesor/a | She is my English teacher. |
| Student | Estudiante | I am a new student. |
| Friend | Amigo/a | He is my friend from university. |
| See you later / Goodbye | Hasta luego / Adios | Goodbye, see you tomorrow! |

### Lección 2 · Gramática

El verbo 'to be' (ser/estar) cambia segun la persona: am, is, are. En español muchas veces no se necesita un verbo para presentarse ('Yo, Camila'), pero en ingles el verbo 'to be' SIEMPRE tiene que aparecer.

**Estructuras:**
- **I** — am / am not / Am I...? _I am Camila. I'm not a teacher. Am I late?_
- **You** — are / aren't / Are you...? _You are a student. You aren't from here. Are you ready?_
- **He / She / It** — is / isn't / Is he...? _She is a teacher. He isn't from Cartagena. Is it far?_
- **We / You / They** — are / aren't / Are we...? _We are friends. They aren't students. Are we late?_

**Error común:** El error mas comun de hispanohablantes es OMITIR el verbo 'to be', porque en español a veces no hace falta ('Yo profesora' en vez de 'I am a teacher'). En ingles el verbo siempre va.

**Práctica dirigida (sin nota, feedback inmediato):**
1. I ___ Camila. — *Respuesta: am*
2. She ___ a teacher. — *Respuesta: is*
3. We _____ from Cartagena. — opciones: am, is, are — *Respuesta: are*
4. _____ you a student? — opciones: Am, Is, Are — *Respuesta: Are*
5. They ___ not from Bogota. — *Respuesta: are*

# A2-01 — Buying and Selling

**Nivel:** A2  
**Gramática:** Be en pasado: afirmativo, negativo y preguntas; count/non-count nouns  
**Vocabulario:** Compras, ventas, productos  
**Can-do:** Puede hablar de compras y situaciones pasadas, describir productos, aceptar/rechazar invitaciones

### Lección 1 · Vocabulario

| Palabra/frase | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| store / shop | tienda | This store has great prices. |
| sale / discount | oferta / descuento | Everything is 20% off during the sale. |
| receipt | recibo | Keep your receipt in case you need a refund. |
| expensive / cheap | caro / barato | That jacket is too expensive for me. |
| refund | reembolso | I asked for a refund because it didn't fit. |
| customer | cliente | The customer was waiting at the counter. |
| cash / card | efectivo / tarjeta | Do you pay by cash or card? |
| to bargain | regatear | You can bargain for a better price at the market. |
| to exchange (an item) | cambiar un producto | Can I exchange this for a bigger size? |
| price / cost | precio / costo | The price went up last month. |
| second-hand | de segunda mano | I bought a second-hand bike. |
| brand new | completamente nuevo | This phone is brand new. |

### Lección 2 · Gramática

El pasado del verbo 'to be' es was/were — funciona igual que am/is/are pero para el pasado. Tambien repasamos sustantivos contables (tienen plural: shirt/shirts) y no contables (no tienen plural: money, no 'moneys') porque en compras se usan constantemente juntos.

**Estructuras:**
- **was/were afirmativo** — I/he/she/it + was; you/we/they + were. _The store was closed yesterday._
- **was/were negativo** — wasn't / weren't. _The shoes weren't expensive._
- **was/were preguntas** — Was/Were + sujeto + ...? _Was the sale good this year?_
- **sustantivos contables** — Tienen plural, se cuentan con numeros: a/an, two, three... _I bought two shirts and a jacket._
- **sustantivos no contables** — No tienen plural. Se usan con some/any/much/a lot of. _I don't have much money left._

**Excepciones:**
- 'You' siempre usa 'were', incluso hablando de una sola persona: 'You were late', nunca 'you was' — error comun por analogia informal.

**Error común:** Usar 'was' con sujetos plurales (they was, we was) o confundir 'money' como contable (a money, moneys) — es no contable.

**Práctica dirigida (sin nota, feedback inmediato):**
1. The store ___ open until 9pm yesterday. — *Respuesta: was*
2. They ___ not happy with the price. — *Respuesta: were*
3. _____ the shoes expensive? — opciones: Was, Were, Is — *Respuesta: Were*
4. I need ___ information before I buy this. — opciones: a, some, many — *Respuesta: some*
5. She bought two ___ (shirt) at the sale. — *Respuesta: shirts*

# B1.1-01 — Education

**Nivel:** B1.1  
**Gramática:** Present Perfect; contraste del Present Perfect con otros tiempos  
**Vocabulario:** Educacion, trabajo  
**Can-do:** Puede hablar de experiencias y acciones pasadas relevantes para el presente y hablar de educacion/trabajo

### Lección 1 · Vocabulario

| Palabra/frase | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| to graduate | graduarse | She graduated from university last year. |
| degree | titulo universitario | He has a degree in engineering. |
| major / field of study | carrera / area de estudio | My major was international business. |
| scholarship | beca | She got a scholarship to study abroad. |
| résumé / CV | hoja de vida | I updated my resume last week. |
| job interview | entrevista de trabajo | I have a job interview tomorrow. |
| to apply for a job | postularse a un empleo | I've applied for five jobs this month. |
| promotion | ascenso | She got a promotion after one year. |
| qualification | cualificacion / requisito | Do you have the qualifications for this role? |
| internship | practica / pasantia | He did an internship at a bank. |
| to enroll | matricularse | I enrolled in an English course. |
| tuition | matricula (costo) | Tuition has increased this year. |
| coursework | trabajo academico del curso | The coursework was very demanding. |
| to hire / to get hired | contratar / ser contratado | She was hired last month. |
| entry-level (job) | puesto de nivel inicial | This is an entry-level position. |

### Lección 2 · Gramática

El Present Perfect (have/has + participio pasado) conecta el pasado con el presente: se usa para experiencias de vida o acciones pasadas cuyo resultado importa AHORA, sin decir exactamente cuando pasaron. Si dices CUANDO exactamente (yesterday, last year, in 2020), tienes que usar Past Simple, no Present Perfect.

**Estructuras:**
- **afirmativo** — have/has + participio. _She has finished her degree._
- **negativo** — haven't/hasn't + participio. _I haven't applied for that job yet._
- **preguntas** — Have/Has + sujeto + participio...? _Have you ever worked abroad?_
- **Present Perfect vs Past Simple** — Present Perfect = sin tiempo especifico / relevante ahora. Past Simple = tiempo especifico mencionado. _I have graduated (en algun momento) vs. I graduated in 2020 (tiempo especifico)._

**Excepciones:**
- 'Ever' y 'never' se usan para hablar de experiencia general en la vida, casi siempre con Present Perfect: 'Have you ever worked in a restaurant?' — nunca con Past Simple con este sentido.
- 'Just', 'already' y 'yet' acompañan frecuentemente al Present Perfect: 'I have just finished my degree' (recien), 'I haven't finished yet' (todavia no).

**Error común:** Usar Present Perfect con una fecha o tiempo especifico: 'I have graduated in 2020' es incorrecto — debe ser 'I graduated in 2020' (Past Simple).

**Práctica dirigida (sin nota, feedback inmediato):**
1. She _____ her degree last year. — opciones: has finished, finished, have finished — *Respuesta: finished*
2. _____ you ever worked abroad? — opciones: Did, Have, Do — *Respuesta: Have*
3. I ___ not started my new job yet. — *Respuesta: have*
4. He _____ for that company since 2019. — opciones: worked, has worked, work — *Respuesta: has worked*
5. They ___ (graduate) two years ago. (Past Simple) — *Respuesta: graduated*

# B2-01 — Couples

**Nivel:** B2  
**Gramática:** Past modals: should have, could have, might have, may have, must have  
**Vocabulario:** Relaciones, parejas, matrimonio, divorcio  
**Can-do:** Puede especular sobre situaciones pasadas, expresar arrepentimiento/critica y formular opiniones sobre acontecimientos pasados

### Lección 1 · Vocabulario

| Palabra/frase | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| to get engaged | comprometerse | They got engaged after five years together. |
| to propose | proponer matrimonio | He proposed to her on the beach. |
| to get married / wedding | casarse / boda | Their wedding is next spring. |
| to break up (with someone) | terminar una relacion | She broke up with him last month. |
| to get divorced / divorce | divorciarse / divorcio | They got divorced after ten years of marriage. |
| to grow apart | distanciarse (emocionalmente) | Over time, they grew apart. |
| to cheat on someone | ser infiel | He never cheated on her, as far as she knows. |
| to make up / reconciliation | reconciliarse | They had a huge fight but made up the next day. |
| trust | confianza | Trust is the foundation of any relationship. |
| jealousy | celos | His jealousy became a real problem. |
| commitment | compromiso (emocional) | She wasn't ready for that level of commitment. |
| ex-partner | ex pareja | Her ex-partner still calls sometimes. |
| second thoughts | dudas de ultimo momento | He had second thoughts right before the wedding. |
| to forgive | perdonar | It took her years to forgive him. |
| argument | discusion / pelea | They had a huge argument about money. |
| soulmate | alma gemela | She believes he is her soulmate. |
| in hindsight | en retrospectiva / visto en retrospectiva | In hindsight, they married too young. |
| to drift apart | alejarse gradualmente | Long distance made them drift apart. |

### Lección 2 · Gramática

Los modales en pasado (modal + have + participio pasado) sirven para hablar de cosas que NO pasaron pero pudieron pasar, para criticar o lamentar decisiones pasadas, o para especular sobre algo que probablemente pasó. No confundir con 'have to' en pasado ('had to'), que es obligacion real, no especulacion.

**Estructuras:**
- **should have + participio** — Critica o arrepentimiento sobre algo que NO se hizo pero debio hacerse. _You should have told me about the wedding. (No me dijiste, y fue un error.)_
- **shouldn't have + participio** — Critica sobre algo que SI se hizo pero fue un error hacerlo. _She shouldn't have canceled the engagement over text. (Lo hizo, y estuvo mal.)_
- **could have + participio** — Una posibilidad o habilidad que existio pero no se uso. _He could have proposed sooner, but he waited two years._
- **might have / may have + participio** — Especulacion incierta sobre el pasado — no estamos seguros. _They might have broken up because of the distance. We're not sure._
- **must have + participio** — Deduccion casi segura basada en evidencia. _She must have forgiven him — they're back together now._

**Excepciones:**
- 'must have' NO se niega con 'mustn't have' para deduccion. Para negar una deduccion casi segura se usa 'can't have' o 'couldn't have': 'He can't have forgotten our anniversary' (NO 'He mustn't have forgotten...').
- 'could have' tambien puede expresar una queja sarcastica ('You could have asked!' = ¡Pudiste haber preguntado!), distinto de su uso como posibilidad neutral — el tono cambia el significado, no la gramatica.

**Registro:** En ingles hablado, estas formas casi siempre se contraen y se pronuncian 'should've' (sueldan como 'shoulda'), 'could've', 'might've'. En escritura formal o academica SIEMPRE se escriben completas: 'should have', nunca 'should of' (error ortografico comun incluso entre hablantes nativos, por la pronunciacion identica a 'should've').

**Error común:** Usar el verbo base en vez del participio: 'She must have go' en vez de 'She must have gone'. Tambien escribir 'should of' en vez de 'should have', copiando la pronunciacion.

**Práctica dirigida (sin nota, feedback inmediato):**
1. You look upset. What happened? — I _____ my phone somewhere. I can't find it. — opciones: must have lost, should have lost, could have lost, must lose — *Respuesta: must have lost*
2. She ___ have told him earlier — now it's too late. (critica) — *Respuesta: should*
3. He wasn't at the party. He _____ had to work late. — opciones: might have, shouldn't have, should have, mustn't have — *Respuesta: might have*
4. I ___ have helped you if you had asked. (oportunidad no aprovechada) — *Respuesta: could*
5. That's not Marta's coat. It _____ have been left by a guest. — opciones: must, should, shouldn't, could of — *Respuesta: must*

Looking back, I think we shouldn't have rushed into wedding planning in the first place without having those conversations. We must have assumed that love alone would sort out the rest.

We got married eventually, in a small ceremony with close family only. It wasn't the wedding either of us had originally planned, but I don't regret the delay. If I hadn't made that phone call, we could have gone through with a wedding neither of us was fully ready for. Sometimes the right decision doesn't look like progress at the time.

2. What did some of her friends think she should have done? — *Respuesta: Spoken up before booking the venue*

### Lección 5 · Writing

**Tipo:** blog post reflection (informal-neutral register) — **Extensión:** 120-180 palabras

**Prompt:** Escribe una entrada de blog (120-180 palabras) reflexionando sobre una decision del pasado — real o inventada — relacionada con una relacion (pareja, amistad o familia). Usa al menos TRES estructuras distintas de modales en pasado (should have, could have, might/may have, must have) para expresar arrepentimiento, especulacion o critica.

**Ejemplo:** _Looking back, I think I could have handled my first serious breakup very differently. At the time, I was convinced I had done nothing wrong, but in hindsight, I probably should have listened more instead of trying to defend myself in every conversation. We must have had the same argument a dozen times without ever really solving anything. I sometimes wonder if we might have stayed together if we'd talked to someone — a friend, a counselor, anyone outside the relationship. We never did, and eventually we just grew apart. I shouldn't have assumed that love was enough to fix everything on its own. These days, I try to remember that a relationship takes more than good intentions._

**Rúbrica:**
- **Cumplimiento de la tarea**: Reflexiona claramente sobre una decision pasada relacionada con una relacion, con una idea central identificable.
- **Uso de modales en pasado**: Usa correctamente al menos 3 estructuras distintas (should have / could have / might-may have / must have) con el participio correcto.
- **Vocabulario de la unidad**: Usa al menos 3 palabras/expresiones de la lista de vocabulario de forma natural.
- **Coherencia y cohesion**: Las ideas fluyen logicamente, con conectores apropiados entre oraciones.
- **Registro**: Mantiene un tono coherente con una entrada de blog personal (semi-informal, reflexivo).
