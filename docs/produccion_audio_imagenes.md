## B2-01 · Couples

### Audio (guarda en `content/B2-01/audio/`)

**Archivo: `B2-01_listening_1.mp3`**
```
A: Did you hear about Marco and Elena? They broke up.
B: What? I thought they were about to get married! What happened?
A: I don't really know the details, but apparently they'd been arguing a lot.
B: That's strange, they always seemed so happy together. She must have been really hurt.
A: Yeah, I heard she found out he'd been talking to his ex-partner again.
B: Talking, or... cheating? Because those are very different things.
A: I'm not sure, honestly. He might have just been catching up with her, or it could have been more than that. Nobody really knows for sure.
B: Well, he shouldn't have kept it a secret either way. That's probably what hurt her the most — not knowing.
A: True. If he'd just told her from the start, she might have understood. Instead, she found out from someone else.
B: That would explain why she's so upset. Trust is hard to rebuild once it's broken like that.
A: Exactly. I heard they tried to make up, but it didn't work out.
B: That's really sad. They seemed like soulmates to everyone else, at least.
A: Sometimes things aren't what they look like from the outside.
```
